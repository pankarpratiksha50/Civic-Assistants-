# Municipal complaint workflow

Suggested workflow statuses are Reported, In Progress, and Resolved. Reported means a complaint has been submitted. In Progress means the complaint has been reviewed and assigned for action. Resolved means the responsible team has marked the work complete. Staff should verify completion before resolving a report.

# Location accuracy

A mobile browser obtains location from the device location services after the user grants permission. Accuracy varies with device hardware, GPS signal, indoor use, and surrounding buildings. The submitted coordinates should be reviewed on a map before dispatching a road crew. Image metadata and device GPS are not proof that a photograph was captured at the reported location.

# Data privacy

Collect only information needed to process the complaint. Do not publish a citizen's email, phone number, or home address on the public map. Protect uploaded images and restrict administrative actions to authorized staff.
