# Pothole reporting — starter guidance

Citizens can report a pothole by taking or uploading a clear road image, enabling device location permission, checking the map pin, and submitting the report. Include a short description and select severity. Do not stand in traffic or put yourself at risk to take a photograph.

# Report review

A report is a request for inspection, not confirmation that road work has been approved. Municipal staff should verify the image and location, check for duplicate reports, assign the responsible road department, and update the status as work progresses.

# Safety

Keep a safe distance from traffic. Do not enter a damaged road or block a vehicle lane to take a photograph. If a road hazard creates immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency or municipal authority using verified official contact information.
